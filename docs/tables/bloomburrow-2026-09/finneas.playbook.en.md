## What the deck is

A green-white Rabbit token deck, and the simplest of the four to pick up.
Finneas costs {G}{W}, has reach and vigilance, and whenever he attacks he puts a
+1/+1 counter on every other creature you control that is a token or a Rabbit;
then, if your creatures' total power is 10 or more, you draw a card. Make many
small Rabbits (Hop to It, Carrot Cake, Head of the Homestead, Intrepid Rabbit),
attack with Finneas every turn so they all grow, and pump the team to finish
(Valley Questcaller, Warren Elder, Rabbit Response, Jazal Goldmane).

## How to pilot it

1. Keep a hand with lands and one-drops. Finneas on turn two is the plan.
2. Turn one: Brave-Kin Duo, Hinterland Sanctifier, Seasoned Warrenguard or a
   mana creature. Turn two: Finneas. Turn three: Hop to It or Valley
   Questcaller or Harvestrite Host.
3. Attack with Finneas every turn it is safe. Vigilance means he still blocks.
   Each attack grows every Rabbit and every token; when total power reaches
   ten, you draw. Count before you attack.
4. Turns four and five: Head of the Homestead, Warren Elder's pump, Innkeeper's
   Talent (at level three every counter is doubled), Banner of Kinship naming
   Rabbit, Heirloom Epic tapping spare tokens for cards.
5. Finish with a pump: Rabbit Response (+2/+1 and scry two), Joraga Invocation
   (+3/+3 and everything must be blocked), Jazal Goldmane's ability (+X/+X
   where X is the number of attackers). For the Common Good copies your best
   token and makes the team indestructible for a turn.
6. Protect the team: Dawn's Truce with the gift promised makes your permanents
   indestructible through a sweeper; Crumb and Get It saves Finneas.
7. Removal: Banishing Light and Prayer of Binding for anything, Parting Gust
   with the gift promised to exile for good, Repel Calamity for big creatures,
   Sonar Strike for attackers, Plummet, Pawpatch Formation and Glorious Decay
   for flyers, Wear Down and Scrapshooter for artifacts and enchantments.

## What to watch

- Alania holds two board wipes. Do not put every token on the table at once;
  keep Hop to It or Head of the Homestead in hand to rebuild, and hold Dawn's
  Truce when she has four mana open.
- Menace Squirrels need two blockers each. You have the bodies; block in pairs.
- Wick's Maha, Its Feathers Night sets your tokens' toughness to one. Answer
  it immediately: Banishing Light, Parting Gust, Angelic Edict, Repel Calamity.
- Parting Gust without the gift returns the creature at end of turn with a
  counter. Promise the gift (the opponent gets a tapped 1/1 Fish) when you
  want it gone.
- Few flyers of your own: Shrike Force, Inspiring Overseer, Dazzling Angel,
  Pileated Provisioner, and Feather of Flight for one more. Finneas, Scrapshooter,
  Barkform Harvester and Clifftop Lookout have reach to block the air.
