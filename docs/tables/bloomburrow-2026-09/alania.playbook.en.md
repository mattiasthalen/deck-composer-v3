# Alania, Divergent Storm — playbook

Deck file: `decks/alania.deck.txt`. Colours: blue-red. Bracket 2.
Every figure below is `check` output for this list; nothing was counted by hand.

## What the deck is

A blue-red spells deck with an Otter core. Whenever you cast the first instant,
the first sorcery, or the first Otter spell (other than Alania) in a turn, you
may let an opponent draw a card; if you do, you copy that spell. Up to three
copies a turn. Around her, Otters and other creatures grow or ping whenever you
cast a noncreature spell: Tempest Angler takes a counter, Coruscation Mage and
Kessig Flamebreather deal a point to each opponent, Kindlespark Duo untaps,
Harnesser of Storms gives a card. This is the hardest of the four decks to
pilot and the one that draws the most cards.

Measured shape:

- 100 cards; 36 lands (17 Island, 14 Mountain, plus 5 nonbasic) against a
  house window of 35–37.
- 26 creatures, the fewest at the table. Average mana value 2.97 (window
  2.5–3.2). Curve by mana value: 1: 6, 2: 24, 3: 16, 4: 8, 5: 6, 6: 2, 7+: 2.
- Otter core: 10 creatures with Otter on the type line and 7 other
  cards that name Otters, 17 in all, out of 18 the collection can
  field in these colours. A tribal core, not a full typal deck. Barkform
  Harvester is a changeling and counts as an Otter in play.
- Draw 21 (target 10, pool ceiling 51), the most at the table.
  Targeted interaction 12 (target 8, ceiling 31).
  Sweepers 2 (target 2, ceiling 3): the table's board wipes
  live here. Ramp 4 (target 10, ceiling 12): Resonating Lute,
  Abstract Paintmage and Hydro-Channeler make spell mana the checker counts;
  Treasures from Blooming Blast it does not.
- Coloured land sources: U 21, R 18.

## How to pilot it

1. Keep a hand with lands, one cheap Otter (Tempest Angler, Kitsa, Stormcatch
   Mentor, Coruscation Mage) and a cheap spell or two.
2. Early turns: an Otter, then spells that replace themselves (Opt, Pearl of
   Wisdom is cheaper with an Otter out, Quick Study, Playful Shove). Each one
   grows Tempest Angler and pings with Coruscation Mage.
3. Cast Alania at five mana. From then on, each turn try to cast one instant,
   one sorcery and one Otter: three copies. The trigger is optional per spell,
   so choose who draws — the player furthest behind, or one whose hand is
   already full.
4. Copy what scales: Divination copied is four cards; Lightning Strike copied
   hits two targets; Otterball Antics copied is two prowess Otters; Stress
   Dream copied is two bolts and two looks.
5. Hold a counter for the one spell that would end you: Negate for
   noncreature, Essence Scatter for creatures, Dazzling Denial as a tax,
   Spellgyre when you can afford four. Maha, Jazal Goldmane's pump, Root
   Manipulation and Rabbit Response are the targets.
6. Sweepers when behind on board: Brotherhood's End (three damage to each
   creature) and Splatter Technique (four, or draw four when the board is
   fine). Your own Otters are small too; count what you lose.
7. Ral, Crackling Wit and Stormchaser's Talent make Otter tokens as you cast;
   Artist's Talent at level two makes every noncreature spell cheaper. Daring
   Waverider and Zealous Lorecaster replay a spent spell.
8. Win with Spectacular Skywhale (+3/+0 for each instant or sorcery), Tolarian
   Terror and Eddymurk Crab (cheap once the graveyard is full of spells), and
   the steady pings from Coruscation Mage, Kessig Flamebreather,
   Thermo-Alchemist and Kindlespark Duo.

## What to watch

- A copy is not cast. Prowess, Tempest Angler and Coruscation Mage trigger on
  the spell you cast, not on the copy.
- Giving cards away is real. Do not feed the player who is racing you; skip
  the trigger when the copy is not worth a card to them.
- Few creatures and almost no ground blockers. Finneas's wide board and
  Camellia's menace Squirrels walk past you; Run Away Together, Dire
  Downdraft and Into the Flood Maw buy turns, sweepers reset.
- Do not run out of gas. Spells are your creatures; Sazacap's Brew, Thrill of
  Possibility and Playful Shove keep the hand full.
- Curse of the Swine exiles X creatures but gives each owner a 2/2 Boar per
  creature; against tokens it is a downgrade, against big creatures a steal.
