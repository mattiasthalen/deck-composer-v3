# Wick, the Whorled Mind — playbook

Deck file: `decks/wick.deck.txt`. Colours: blue-black-red. Bracket 2.
Every figure below is `check` output for this list; nothing was counted by hand.

## What the deck is

A Rat deck that feeds a Snail. Whenever Wick or another Rat enters under your
control, you either make a 1/1 black Snail token (if you have none) or put a
+1/+1 counter on a Snail you already have. Then, for {U}{B}{R}, Wick sacrifices
a Snail: it deals damage equal to the Snail's power to each opponent and you
draw that many cards. The whole deck is built to make Rats enter often, grow one
Snail, and cash it in.

Measured shape:

- 100 cards; 36 lands (11 Island, 7 Mountain, 11 Swamp, plus 7 nonbasic) against a
  house window of 35–37.
- 33 creatures. Average mana value 3.14 (window 2.5–3.2). Curve by
  mana value: 1: 3, 2: 20, 3: 19, 4: 11, 5: 9, 6: 2.
- Rat core: 13 creatures with Rat on the type line and 4 other
  cards that name Rats, 17 in all, out of 17 the collection can
  field in these colours. That is a tribal core, not a full typal deck: the rest
  of the list is removal, card draw and support creatures. Barkform Harvester
  and Three Tree Mascot are changelings, so they count as Rats in play even
  though the checker does not count them in the core.
- Draw 15 (target 10, pool ceiling 62). Targeted interaction
  16 (target 8, ceiling 51). Ramp 3 (target 10,
  ceiling 13): the checker does not count Treasure makers, so Rapacious
  Dragon, Corsair Captain and Seize the Spoils are extra mana it does not see.
  Sweepers 0 (target 2, ceiling 3): a build choice, because
  Wick's own board is small creatures and the collection's sweepers went to
  Alania.
- Coloured land sources: U 15, B 14, R 10.

## How to pilot it

1. Keep a hand with three or four lands and a Rat that costs two or three.
   Blue and black matter first; red is for removal and for the Snail
   activation later.
2. Early turns: cheap Rats (Shoreline Looter, Thought Shucker, Azure
   Beastbinder, Persistent Marshstalker). Take every chance to put cards in
   your graveyard (Daggerfang Duo, Diresight, Mindwhisker's surveil, Psychic
   Whorl): several Rats get much better once the graveyard holds seven cards
   or more (that is what "threshold" means on the cards).
3. Cast Wick as soon as you have four mana with a black source. From then on
   every Rat that enters grows the Snail. Pay offspring on Thornplate
   Intimidator for two Rat entries. Skyskipper Duo, Long River Lurker, Dour
   Port-Mage, Splash Portal and Gossip's Talent (level three) re-enter a Rat
   for another trigger.
4. Sacrifice the Snail when it has power four or more, or whenever it is about
   to die anyway. Damage to every opponent plus that many cards is the engine;
   never lose a big Snail to a removal spell you could have pre-empted.
5. Spend removal on the creature that hurts you most, not the biggest one.
   Hero's Downfall, Feed the Cycle, Fell, Nocturnal Hunger and Foolish Fate
   kill; Early Winter and Wander Off exile; Lightning Strike, Take Out the
   Trash and Agate Assault finish small utility creatures; Dire Downdraft and
   Into the Flood Maw are tempo answers.
6. Hand attack (Psychic Whorl, Thought-Stalker Warlock, Bandit's Talent,
   Cruelclaw's Heist) is best aimed at whoever is holding the most cards,
   usually Alania.
7. Maha, Its Feathers Night is the closer. Every creature your opponents
   control gets base toughness 1, so any -1/-1 effect (Savor, Downwind
   Ambusher) or any damage kills it, and your blockers trade up.

## What to watch

- The Snail is one body. If it is killed, or Wick is removed, the engine
  stops; keep a Rat in hand to restart it rather than dumping everything.
- No board wipe. A wide token board (Finneas) or menace Squirrels (Camellia)
  must be answered one at a time or raced in the air with the flyers.
- Threshold takes time in a hundred-card deck. Do not plan around unblockable
  Rats before the graveyard is at seven.
- Three colours on basics. Hold Uncharted Haven for the colour you lack; Wick
  costs {3}{B}, and his activation needs one of each colour.
- Alania holds counterspells. Bait one with a lesser spell before you commit
  Maha or a gifted Coiling Rebirth.
- Vampiric Tutor is deliberately not in the deck: it is the one Game Changer
  in the collection and this table is Bracket 2.
