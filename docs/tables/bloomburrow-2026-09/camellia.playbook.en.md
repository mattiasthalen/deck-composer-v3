# Camellia, the Seedmiser — playbook

Deck file: `decks/camellia.deck.txt`. Colours: black-green. Bracket 2.
Every figure below is `check` output for this list; nothing was counted by hand.

## What the deck is

A Squirrel deck that runs on Food. Camellia has menace and gives every other
Squirrel menace. Whenever you sacrifice one or more Foods you make a 1/1 green
Squirrel token. For {2} and a forage — exile three cards from your graveyard,
or sacrifice a Food — Camellia puts a +1/+1 counter on each other Squirrel.
Food comes from Bakersbane Duo, Vinereap Mentor, Bumbleflower's Sharepot,
Heaped Harvest, Savor, Cache Grab and Scavenger's Talent; the graveyard fills
from Cache Grab, Diresight, Daggerfang Duo and Stargaze. Sacrifice, grow,
attack with a team that needs two blockers each.

Measured shape:

- 100 cards; 36 lands (16 Forest, 15 Swamp, plus 5 nonbasic) against a

- 33 creatures. Average mana value 2.78 (window 2.5–3.2). Curve by

  collection's board wipes are all red, so black-green cannot have one.

## How to pilot it

1. Keep a hand with lands plus a mana creature or a Food maker. Elvish
   Mystic, Llanowar Elves, Thornvault Forager and Three Tree Rootweaver are
   the mana creatures.
2. Early turns: mana creature, then Bakersbane Duo or Vinereap Mentor for a
   Food, or Bonecache Overseer. Cast Camellia on turn three or as soon as you
   have black and green.
3. Then Squirrels every turn. Honored Dreyleader grows with each Squirrel or
   Food that enters; Corpseberry Cultivator forages at each of your combats;
   Bushy Bodyguard with offspring is two bodies; Treetop Sentries and Curious
   Forager pay you for foraging.
4. Sacrifice Food on purpose, not only for life: each sacrifice is a Squirrel
   token from Camellia. Feed the Cycle's extra cost, Thornvault Forager's
   mana ability and Camellia's own ability all forage.
5. Pump before attacking: Camellia's {2} at the start of combat, Root
   Manipulation for a lethal swing (+2/+2, menace and life on each attacker).
6. Removal is deep; do not hoard it. Grapple with Death, Feed the Cycle, Fell,
   Nocturnal Hunger, Bitter Triumph, Consumed by Greed and Early Winter for
   creatures; Pawpatch Formation, Glorious Decay and Plummet-style effects for
   flyers; Scrapshooter, Reclamation Sage, Hivespine Wolverine and Witherbloom
   Charm for artifacts and enchantments. Wick's Snail and Alania's engine
   creatures are the best targets.
7. Get value back: Bonebind Orator and Curious Forager return cards, Osteomancer
   Adept lets you cast creatures from the graveyard by foraging, Zombify and
   Hazel's Nocturne recover the best ones. Huskburster Swarm gets cheaper for
   every creature card you own in the graveyard or in exile — foraging feeds it.

## What to watch

- Forage competes with recursion. Exiling three cards is free only if you do
  not want them back; sacrifice a Food instead when the graveyard holds a
  creature you want to Zombify.
- Camellia is a three-mana commander with no built-in protection and she dies
  often. Recast her; Overprotect saves her once.
- Almost no flyers (Glidedive Duo is the one) and no board wipe. Finneas's and
  Wick's flyers get through: keep one flyer answer in hand.
- Life is a resource here. Diresight, Bonecache Overseer, Stargaze and
  Nocturnal Hunger (without the gift) cost life; Food, Pest Mascot and
  Shopkeeper's Bane give it back.
- Professor Dellian Fel is a planeswalker: use the zero ability for cards, the
  minus for a creature, and protect it with the menace team.
